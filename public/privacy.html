Legal

Privacy policy

Last updated [DATE]

This practice is committed to protecting your privacy. As a private health service provider I am bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, and by the Health Records and Information Privacy Act 2002 (NSW) and its Health Privacy Principles.

What I collect

  • Your name and contact details
  • Health information relevant to your care, including relevant diagnoses, medications and daily function
  • Funding and billing details — NDIS participant number and plan management details, private health fund details, or DVA details
  • Correspondence with people involved in your care

How I collect it

Directly from you, and from people you have authorised — such as your referring practitioner, a family member, or your support coordinator.

Why I collect it

To assess your needs, plan and deliver occupational therapy, communicate with people involved in your care, invoice correctly, and meet my legal and professional obligations. If you choose not to provide some information, I may not be able to deliver a safe or effective service.

Who I disclose it to

With your consent, I share relevant information with your referring practitioner, other practitioners involved in your care, and your plan manager or funding body for invoicing. I disclose information without your consent only where the law requires or permits it — for example where there is a serious and imminent risk to someone's life, health or safety, or where I am compelled by a court.

Storage and security

Clinical records are held securely on encrypted storage within Australia, with access restricted to me. I do not store health information in marketing systems, spreadsheets or unsecured email.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising technology and no third-party fonts or scripts. Nothing is loaded from another company's servers, and no record of your visit is created by me. There is no contact form on this site — enquiries are made through a separate secure form, which has its own handling of the information you submit.

Enquiries

Please don't include detailed health information in an enquiry. A sentence about what you would like help with is enough to start a conversation; clinical detail is gathered securely once you are booked in.

Retention

Health records are retained for the minimum periods required under NSW law — including the longer period that applies where a person was under 18 at their last appointment — and then securely destroyed.

Access and correction

You may request access to your health information, or ask me to correct it, at any time. I will respond within 45 calendar days.

Complaints

If you are concerned about how I have handled your information, please tell me first through the enquiry form. You may also contact:

  • NSW Information and Privacy Commission — 1800 472 679, ipc.nsw.gov.au
  • Office of the Australian Information Commissioner — 1300 363 992, oaic.gov.au

If you need urgent support, this practice is not an emergency service. Contact your GP, call Lifeline on 13 11 14, or attend your nearest emergency department.

This website provides general information about an occupational therapy service. It is not clinical advice and does not create a therapeutic relationship. Whether occupational therapy is appropriate for you is determined through individual assessment.
