The process

How it works.

The whole path, so you know what you are agreeing to before you agree to any of it.

From enquiry to written summary

  1. You get in touch

    A sentence about what you'd like help with is enough. I reply within [2] business days, and I'll tell you plainly if I think someone else is a better fit.

  2. A short questionnaire

    I send a few questions about a typical day and what you've already tried. Answer what you want to — partial answers are genuinely fine, and they save session time either way.

  3. Initial session

    [60] minutes, in person or by video. We work out what is actually getting in the way, rather than what it looks like from the outside.

  4. Findings and goals

    I set out what I think is happening and why, in plain language. Goals get written in your words, not clinical ones, and they are what we measure against later.

  5. A short block of sessions

    Usually [4–6]. Practical work between sessions, sized to what a bad week can absorb rather than a good one.

  6. Review, and a written summary

    We look at the original goals in their original wording and decide together whether to continue, pause, or stop. You get a short written summary of what we did and what helped.

Where sessions happen

In person in Sydney, and by video across New South Wales. Telehealth is a genuine option rather than a fallback — much of this work is about your actual home and routines. The consulting address is confirmed when you book.
