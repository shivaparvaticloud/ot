Simple Roots Therapy provides holistic and considered Occupational Therapy, coaching, group programs and mentoring throughout Sydney for paediatric, adolescent and adult populations.

We would love to hear how we can best support you. Crystal strongly believes in honest, integral offerings, be it therapy or coaching. If Crystal feels she is not the best fit for you, she will tell you honestly.

What to include

  • Who the therapy is for, and their age
  • A sentence or two on what you are hoping to work on
  • Whether you are looking at NDIS, private health, or paying privately
  • Whether you would prefer clinic, home, school or telehealth sessions

Please do not send detailed health information by email, as email is not a secure channel. A short summary is all that is needed to work out whether we are the right fit, and clinical detail is gathered securely once you are booked in.

If you need urgent support, this practice is not an emergency service. Please contact your GP, call Lifeline on 13 11 14, or attend your nearest emergency department.

This website provides general information about an occupational therapy service. It is not clinical advice and does not create a therapeutic relationship. Whether occupational therapy is appropriate for you is determined through individual assessment.
