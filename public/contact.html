What to include

  • A sentence or two on what you'd like help with
  • Whether you're looking at NDIS, private health, or paying privately
  • Whether you'd prefer to meet in person or by video
  • Anything that would make a first session easier for you

Please don't send detailed health information in an enquiry. Clinical detail is gathered securely once you're booked in — a short summary is all that's needed to work out whether this is the right service.

If occupational therapy isn't the right fit

Say so in your enquiry if you're unsure. I'd rather point you somewhere better early than take a booking that won't help, and there's no cost to asking.

This is not an emergency service. If you need urgent support, contact your GP, call Lifeline on 13 11 14, or attend your nearest emergency department.

This website provides general information about an occupational therapy service. It is not clinical advice and does not create a therapeutic relationship. Whether occupational therapy is appropriate for you is determined through individual assessment.
