Sessions

How it works.

Finding the right therapist for you and your family is important. Your journey is designed so that everyone can be sure of the right fit before intervention begins.

From first contact to review

  1. Enquiry

    Email us to share what you are looking for. A few sentences is enough. No referral is needed, and we will get back to you promptly.

  2. Free 10-minute phone consultation

    Crystal will get in touch directly via telephone for a warm introductory conversation. This is a chance to introduce ourselves and make sure Crystal is the right fit for you. You’ll discuss your needs, ask any questions, and make sure our services align with your goals before scheduling an in-person session.

  3. Initial assessment

    We’ll consult in-person for a longer first session to understand the full picture. This first session is mostly focused on getting to know you and your child, what your situation is and how best you’d like support. Crystal will share honestly if she is not the best fit for you; if that is the case, your assessment will not be billed at the full rate.

  4. Findings and goals

    Crystal sets out what she believes is happening and why, in plain language. Goals are written in your words, and they are what progress is measured against.

  5. Ongoing sessions

    Regular sessions, including coaching for parents and carers, so that strategies carry into daily life rather than staying in the clinic.

  6. Review

    Progress is reviewed against the original goals, and you decide together whether to continue, pause or finish.

Session types

Free phone consultation
Ten minutes, at no charge, before anything is scheduled.
Initial assessment
An in-depth first assessment using formal and informal assessment tools to understand your full picture. This is typically 1–1.5 hours face to face.
Standard session
Ongoing weekly sessions to progress along your goals. This is typically 60 minutes face-to-face, with 10 minutes clinical record and planning time. Sessions take place at the clinic and, if relevant, within your home or community setting.

Delivery modes

  • In-clinic sessions
  • Home visits, where the home environment is central to the goals
  • School and preschool visits, with observation and educator liaison
  • Telehealth by secure video, including parent and carer coaching

Funding options

All services are GST free.

NDIS
Self-managed and plan-managed participants, charged in line with the current NDIS Pricing Arrangements, under a signed service agreement. Agency-managed participants cannot currently be invoiced directly.
Private health
A receipt with a provider number is issued for you to claim, if your extras cover includes occupational therapy.
Private
Paying privately, which is often best suited to adolescent and adult coaching services.

Appointments cancelled with less notice, and missed appointments, are charged in full, because the time cannot be reallocated — waived where there is a genuine emergency or genuine illness.
