Terms of service

Last updated [DATE]

What this service is

Occupational therapy is a registered health service. I work with you on daily function — routines, attention and task management, sensory strategies, arousal regulation and daily structure. I hold general registration with the Occupational Therapy Board of Australia.

What this service is not

This is not psychological therapy, psychiatric care, diagnosis of a mental health condition, or crisis support. If you need urgent help, contact your GP, call Lifeline on 13 11 14, or attend your nearest emergency department. I will tell you if I believe your needs sit outside my scope of practice, and will help you find an appropriate referral.

Outcomes

Occupational therapy is a collaborative process. Progress depends on many factors including your circumstances and what happens between sessions. I do not guarantee any particular outcome.

Fees and payment

Current fees are listed on the home page. Payment is due [at the time of the appointment / within 7 days of invoice]. Occupational therapy provided as appropriate treatment is GST-free. If you hold private health extras cover that includes occupational therapy, I can issue a receipt with my provider number for you to claim.

NDIS participants who are plan-managed or self-managed are charged at or below the NDIS Pricing Schedule maximum prices, under a separate signed service agreement.

Cancellations

Please give at least [24 / 48] hours notice to change or cancel an appointment. Appointments cancelled with less notice, and missed appointments, are charged at [XX]% of the session fee. I waive this where there is a genuine emergency.

For NDIS participants, the NDIS short-notice cancellation rules apply and take precedence over this clause. I will always let you know before claiming a cancellation.

Records and privacy

I keep clinical records as required by law and store them securely. Your information is handled in accordance with the Privacy Policy, the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Health Records and Information Privacy Act 2002 (NSW).

Communication

I respond to email and phone messages within [2] business days during business hours. Email is not secure and should not be used for clinical or urgent matters.

Telehealth

Telehealth sessions are delivered over a secure video connection. Telehealth is not appropriate for every person or every purpose, and I will discuss with you whether it suits your situation.

Your rights

Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Concerns

If you are unhappy with any part of this service, please tell me — I would rather know. You may also contact AHPRA on 1300 419 495, the NSW Health Care Complaints Commission on 1800 043 159, or, for NDIS supports, the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission on 1800 035 544. Raising a concern will not affect the service you receive.
