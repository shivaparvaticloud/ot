About

Meet your therapist.

Crystal is an experienced Occupational Therapist working with children, adolescents and adults in both individual and group settings. She loves her work.

Whether you are a teenager struggling with motivation and discipline in fulfilling your goals, or a child learning to balance and regulate, seeing individuals reach their potential, break perceived barriers and transform lifestyles inspires her daily.

Clinical interests

  • Attention and concentration
  • Emotional regulation
  • Long term behavioural change and habit formation
  • Life skills coaching
  • Gross and fine motor skills
  • Parent coaching

Therapeutic approach

Crystal believes therapy should be collaborative and inspiring. Good therapy empowers individuals and families rather than lead to dependency and complacency. Her therapeutic approach is warm, holistic and lifestyle focused.

She recognises that our environments play a profound role in our ways of being and development. In her sessions you will find a focus not only on the individual, but also on the surrounding home, familial, social, environmental and lifestyle context. Her aim is to equip individuals and families with the tools and strategies needed to take therapy out of the clinic and integrate it into daily life.

Beyond the clinic

Having a deep interest in mindfulness and traditional healing methods, Crystal is also a certified Meditation Teacher and Pranic Healer. Outside of the clinic, she collaborates with communities and not-for-profit organisations, teaching courses and programs on mindfulness, yoga and holistic living. She is a passionate teacher of Sanskrit, Eastern philosophical texts and meditation. Otherwise, you will find her exploring natural reserves, painting, or mentoring adolescents and adults in ayurvedic practices.

Our values

Honesty
Transparency and open dialogue create a clear, trusted environment, to ensure true, inspiring and measurable progress.
Collaboration
Growth is a shared commitment. Crystal believes progress is best achieved in unison, combining your lived experience with clinical expertise to progress along your highest ideals.
Excellence
Therapists hold a profound responsibility to deliver their best. Moving beyond transactional practice, Crystal commits to genuine effort, deep clinical consideration, continuous learning and honest self-review.
Self-actualisation & determination
Every individual deserves to live as their most authentic, confident self. Crystal’s approach will always prioritise your growing autonomy and resilience, rather than a dependence on the therapist.
