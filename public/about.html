About

A small practice, deliberately.

Simple Occupational Therapy is a solo practice. You enquire, and the person who replies is the person you see — there is no intake team and no handover.

Who you'd be working with

My name is [YOUR NAME]. I'm an occupational therapist working with adults and adolescents, in person in Sydney and by video across New South Wales.

I hold general registration with the Occupational Therapy Board of Australia. Registration can be checked by name on the public register at ahpra.gov.au, which is the authoritative source — worth doing for any health practitioner you're considering.

Why the practice runs this way

Most of the people I see have already tried a great deal, often for years, and have been handed strategies that assume a level of baseline capacity they don't currently have. The work here starts from what a bad week looks like rather than a good one.

That principle shows up in small decisions. Blocks of sessions are short and reviewed rather than open-ended. Homework is sized to be done badly and still count. And you're told what I think is going on, in plain language, rather than being managed.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party fonts or scripts, and no contact form. Nothing is loaded from another company's servers, so no record of your visit is created here. For a health service, that seemed like the right default rather than a feature.

Group program

I run a small group program on attention and daily routines a few times a year. Enquire to hear when the next one opens — nothing else, there's no newsletter.
