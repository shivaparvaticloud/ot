Our services

Occupational therapy, assessment and coaching.

Simple Roots Therapy provides Occupational Therapy and assessments, coaching and group programs throughout Sydney for paediatric, adolescent and adult populations.

What is occupational therapy?

Occupational therapy is a registered health profession concerned with function — what an individual truly does across a day, and what makes that harder or easier. “Occupation” does not mean a job. It means any activity that occupies our time: getting up, getting dressed, playing, learning, working, connecting, resting and sleeping.

An occupational therapist looks at the person, the task and the environment together, because difficulty usually sits in the fit between them rather than in the person alone.

Paediatric services

Ages 2–12 years, with or without a formal diagnosis.

True progress happens at home! Crystal strongly believes paediatric therapy works best as a partnership with carers. That means, your paediatric therapy will be child-led, play-based and relationship-centric. It may also include home and school visits and intervention.

Beyond engaging directly with your child, Crystal coaches parents and caregivers to deeply understand and support their child’s unique needs. This is important to her — she believes therapy should empower you to bring strategies out of short weekly sessions and into the everyday, where true life happens. This ensures meaningful, long-lasting progress, rather than relying solely on clinic visits that can leave your child and family dependent.

We have particular experience with

  • Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)
  • ADHD (Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder)
  • Sensory Processing Differences
  • Global Developmental Delay (GDD)
  • Gross motor difficulties
  • Fine motor difficulties
  • Attention and concentration difficulties
  • Emotional regulation difficulties
  • Early childhood trauma
  • Self care activities

Our Practice is shaped by

Ayres Sensory Integration
Effective sensory processing and integration underpin a child’s development, and serve as a foundation for learning.
Attachment Theory
Early relationships shape emotional, social and cognitive development, and provide a path to building skills.
Circle of Security
A relationship-based framework for reading a child’s needs and responding to them consistently.
CO-OP Approach
Cognitive Orientation to daily Occupational Performance — guided strategy discovery, so skills generalise beyond the session.
DIR Model
Developmental, Individual-differences, Relationship-based — relationships and emotional connection strengthen developmental capacities.
Internal Family Systems
Parts theory, used to understand the different internal drivers behind behaviour.
Play Theory
Play is both a therapeutic tool and a developmental goal, supporting engagement, regulation and skill building.
Polyvagal Theory
The nervous system plays a critical role in regulation and performance, placing attunement and co-regulation at the heart of intervention.

Adult and adolescent services

Understanding why we choose what we do turns daily habits into life-changing transformation.

When working with adults and adolescents, Crystal takes a coaching approach. She has a special interest in habit formation and executive functioning (planning, initiating, organising and executing tasks). Together, you’ll be supported to move beyond procrastination and unintentional dis-organisation through metacognition, skills building and structural change within your home and work environment.

Working in both private, corporate and therapeutic settings, she helps you step back and take a metacognitive look at your habits, mindset and environments. Together you will build strategies, action plans and accountability systems to create the change you are after.

This self-determined holistic approach bridges the gap between intention and action. You’ll be guided to create practical strategies that integrate into your lifestyle for clear goal attainment and a truer sense of self.
