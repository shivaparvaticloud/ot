What we believe

Simple Roots Therapy is dedicated to a world of intentional, inspired and conscious beings, families and communities. We believe every individual deserves to live life as their fullest, brightest and most confident selves.

Through knowing our own minds and body, creating a supportive physical environment, and building upon meaningful and fulfilling relationships, we can establish the simple roots needed for a fulfilling and harmonious life.

The three simple roots

Self

Knowing your own mind and body — attention, regulation, sensory needs and the physical skills a day asks for.

Environment

The spaces you live and work in, and the routines that run through them. A supportive environment carries a great deal of the load.

Others

Meaningful, fulfilling relationships — family, carers, educators and community — and the support they can offer when they understand what helps.
